Quran Tajweed

Privacy Policy · Effective 9 November 2026

In short

Quran Tajweed does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you save in the app stays on your phone. This app is suitable for all ages, including children, and works the same for everyone.

What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, notes, reading position, Hifz settings, Tasbeeh counts, prayer settings and city are saved only on your device. The developer never receives them. If your phone's own Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your private Google backup; the developer cannot see that backup. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhyIs anything sent anywhere?
Microphone Only in Recite mode, after you tap the microphone, to check your recitation word by word. No. The speech check runs fully on your phone. Your voice is never uploaded or saved on a server. The recording is kept on the phone only so you can play it back, and is left out of Android backups.
Approximate location Only if you tap “Use my location”, to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. You can instead pick your city from the built-in list. No. The calculation is done on your phone with a built-in city list. Your location is never sent. The app does not ask for precise location.
Notifications Prayer alerts, the reminder before prayer, the Ayah of the day, audio controls and download progress. No.
Alarms & reminders, run at start-up So prayer alerts come on time and are set again after the phone restarts. No.
Internet To play recitation audio, download surahs for offline listening, and download the Recite-mode speech model once (only after you agree). The app only downloads files. No personal information is sent. See “Internet services” below.

Internet services the app downloads from

When you play or download audio, your phone requests the audio files from these public servers: everyayah.com, download.quranicaudio.com, audio.qurancdn.com (Quran.com). When you agree to download the Recite-mode speech model, it comes from huggingface.co. Like any website, these servers receive the technical information every internet request carries (such as your IP address) so they can send the file back. The app sends them nothing else, and the developer has no access to their logs. Their own privacy policies apply to those servers.

Sharing

When you choose Share on an ayah, the text or picture goes only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). The developer does not see it.

Children

The app is made for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, does not show ads, has no chat or social features, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
